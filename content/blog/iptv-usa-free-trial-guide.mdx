## 2. Red Flags and Dark Patterns in the IPTV Industry

Unfortunately, shady resellers frequently use deceptive "trial" offers as bait to trap unsuspecting consumers. Watch out for these three major industry red flags:

### The "Free" Trial That Requires a Credit Card
If a website advertises a "Free 7-Day Trial" but demands your credit card number upfront, proceed with extreme caution. These operations rely on dark billing patterns, hoping you forget to navigate a deliberately confusing cancellation process so they can automatically bill you for an expensive annual subscription. A true trial is always 100% card-free.

### Artificial Trial Bandwidth Prioritization
Some questionable providers maintain special "trial servers" with abundant bandwidth to trick prospective buyers during their test window. Once you buy a long-term plan, your account gets silently shifted to overloaded production servers where constant buffering begins. To protect yourself, always test your trial during peak viewing hours—such as Sunday afternoon football or evening prime-time broadcasts—to observe real-world performance under full network load.

### WhatsApp-Only Disappearing Acts
Be wary of services that operate exclusively through anonymous social media groups or temporary chat numbers without an official web portal or published terms of service. If a provider cannot invest in professional web infrastructure, they will not be around to support your account when major sporting tournaments kick off.
