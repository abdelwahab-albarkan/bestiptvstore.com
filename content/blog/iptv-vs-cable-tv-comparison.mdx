## 1. The Real Cost of Cable: Exposing Hidden Fees

When cable companies advertise a "$79.99 monthly bundle," the promotional number on the billboard bears almost no resemblance to your final monthly credit card charge. Once promotional discounts expire after 12 months, legacy cable bills routinely balloon to $160, $200, or even $250 every month.

The primary culprit behind skyrocketing cable costs is a barrage of mandatory administrative fees that cannot be removed:
- **Broadcast TV Fee ($25 to $35/month):** Cable companies charge you an extra surcharge simply to retransmit local channels like CBS, NBC, ABC, and FOX—stations that transmit over the public airwaves for free.
- **Regional Sports Network (RSN) Fee ($15 to $22/month):** Even if you never watch a single baseball or basketball game, cable providers force subscribers to subsidize regional sports broadcast contracts.
- **Set-Top Box and DVR Rental Fees ($10 to $15/box/month):** If your home has a living room TV, a master bedroom TV, and a basement TV, you pay upwards of $40 every month just to rent plastic hardware that the cable company already amortized years ago.
- **Regulatory Franchise Fees and Taxes ($12 to $18/month):** Local municipality taxes compounded on top of inflated base rates.

When you total these expenses, the average American household spends **between $1,900 and $2,700 every year** just to watch basic television.

## 5. Video Quality and Internet Speed Requirements

A frequent concern among prospective cord-cutters is whether their home internet connection is fast enough to handle IPTV streaming.

Legacy cable typically delivers MPEG-2 or compressed 720p/1080i signals over aging copper coaxial lines, which often look muddy on modern 4K OLED televisions. Modern IPTV leverages advanced **HEVC (H.265) and 60FPS video compression**, producing razor-sharp 1080p, 4K Ultra HD, and select 8K broadcasts with vibrant HDR color grading.

To enjoy buffer-free streaming, you do not need gigabit fiber:
- **HD Streams (1080p):** Requires 10 to 15 Mbps download speed.
- **4K Ultra HD Streams:** Requires 25 to 35 Mbps download speed.
- **8K Feeds:** Requires 50 Mbps stable broadband.

With the average US home broadband speed currently exceeding 150 Mbps according to FCC data, virtually every American household already possesses more than enough internet speed to replace cable entirely.
