## IPTV for Windows PC: Hardware Decoding & Player Setup

Configuring VLC with Direct3D11 hardware acceleration offloads high-bitrate video decompression from your CPU to your dedicated GPU for smooth 60FPS streaming.

### Windows DirectX Hardware Decoding

Enabling automatic hardware decoding in player preferences eliminates audio desynchronization and frame lag on high-definition sports feeds.

### VLC Network Caching & Buffer Optimization

Setting your VLC network caching buffer to 3000ms absorbs minor internet latency variations, preventing video stuttering during prime-time hours.
